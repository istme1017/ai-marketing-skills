### Tailoring notes (delete before sending)

**The problem this version solves is overqualification.** For associate roles you are not competing on credentials — you have more than the job asks for. You are competing against the hiring manager's suspicion that you will leave in three months. Everything here is built to defuse that:

- **Reframes Process Assistant as hands-on floor work**, which is true — PAs work the floor — rather than as management. A resume that reads "led 80–100 associates, owned KPIs, drove cross-functional strategy" applied to a picker role tells the recruiter you are aiming somewhere else.
- **Drops the "Seeking to return to Amazon as an Area Manager" line.** That sentence is correct on Tracks A–C and actively disqualifying here.
- **Leads the summary with reliability and physical capability**, the two things that actually get associate applications advanced.
- **Adds an explicit availability block.** Associate hiring screens on shift flexibility more than on background.
- **Keeps the CS degree** — no reason to hide it, and Amazon lists a bachelor's as *preferred* for the Delivery Station CS Associate role.

**Expect the interview question "why are you applying for this role?"** Have a straight answer ready. The honest one is strong: you want back inside Amazon, you know the building and the standards, and you are willing to re-enter at associate level to do it. Recruiters respond well to that when it is said plainly — what they react badly to is a candidate who pretends the gap in level is not there.
