## Read this first: what I did and did not do

**I could not submit the applications.** I built everything up to the submit button, but I cannot click it. Three concrete reasons:

1. The Indeed tool I have access to is **search-only** — `search_jobs`, `get_job_details`, `get_resume`. There is no apply endpoint.
2. Submitting requires being **logged into your accounts** (Indeed, `amazon.jobs`, Workday, Taleo). I run in an isolated container with no session and no credentials, and you should not send them to me.
3. Amazon and most large employers gate applications behind **per-application questionnaires, work-authorization and shift-availability attestations, and sometimes assessments**. Those are legal declarations that need to be you.

So this is the same thing the TikTok workflow actually produces. Watch it again — steps 1–10 are *search → screen → insert into tracker → generate tailored resumes → write summary report*. Nothing in that workflow submits an application either. The title says "search for 100 jobs I can apply to," not "apply for me." What that setup automates is the finding and prep; a human still clicks apply.

**What you get here:** 24 pre-screened roles ranked by fit, with direct apply links and three tailored resume versions. Realistically 15–25 minutes to work through the top 10.

## Before you start clicking

Four things that will cost you interviews if you skip them:

1. **Your Indeed profile says your preferred job title is "It."** That is a typo and it is actively steering Indeed's recommendations and recruiter searches away from operations roles. Fix it to *Area Manager / Operations Supervisor* at https://profile.indeed.com before applying.
2. **Your Indeed profile resume is much thinner than your PDF** — it lists job titles with no accomplishment bullets and no quantified results. Upload the PDF or paste in Track A. Recruiters search the profile, not your attachment.
3. **Your resume PDF lists four Amazon references with their `@amazon.com` addresses on the document itself.** Take them off and replace with "References available on request." Publishing colleagues' work emails on a document you send to strangers exposes them to spam and phishing, and it is not standard practice. All three tailored versions already do this.
4. **The Police Academy Training date reads Jul 2026** — one month ago, and after your Amazon role ended in Jan 2026. If that is correct, good, it is current. If it is a typo for 2019, fix it: as written it looks like an error and recruiters will read it as carelessness.
